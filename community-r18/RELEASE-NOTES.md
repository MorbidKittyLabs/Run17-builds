# Morbid Kitty — STFC Community

Open **MorbidKitty-Setup** and click **INSTALL**, then launch from the Morbid Kitty desktop shortcut.

- Borderless play and a movable controller with a clear background.
- One-click ship shortcuts, clock and six named timers.
- Chat voice typing, Store, Save Settings and Diagnostics.
- F8 restores controls; F9 switches to the desktop window; F10 closes STFC.

Built on STFC Community Mod by NetNiV and contributors, with settings-saving contributions from Guffawaffle/stfc-mod. Morbid Kitty additions by Morbid Kitty Labs.

GPL-3.0. Source, scan reports and checksums accompany the build.
