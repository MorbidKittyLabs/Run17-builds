# Morbid Kitty — STFC

Open **MorbidKitty-Setup** and click **INSTALL**, then launch from the Morbid Kitty desktop shortcut.

- Full-monitor black Morbid Kitty splash waits for the game window to settle.
- Smaller controller: clock, Timers, Diagnostics, Store, microphone and Save.
- White Xs in red/purple boxes, a green restore arrow, and right-drag through controller gaps.
- Numbered ship shortcuts follow the docks; controls follow game UI scaling and loading transitions.
- Microphone toggles chat and Windows voice typing together.
- Borderless movable timer editor, six named timers with days/hours/minutes/seconds.
- Persistent centered completion notices and queued chirp/“Program complete” audio.
- F8 restores controls; F9 switches borderless/windowed play; F10 closes STFC.

Built on STFC Community Mod by NetNiV and contributors, with settings-saving contributions from Guffawaffle/stfc-mod. Morbid Kitty additions by Morbid Kitty Labs.
